Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the SMS Change Order System ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information submitted via SMS.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your mobile phone number and the content of SMS messages you send to our system for the purpose of logging construction change orders.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your phone number and message content are used solely to:

  • Create and log change order records in our project management system
  • Send you confirmation replies via SMS
  • Alert our team to errors or issues via internal notifications

3. SMS Messaging

By texting our number, you consent to receive SMS replies confirming your change order submission. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information or message content to any third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

Change order records are retained for project documentation purposes. Phone numbers are not stored beyond what is necessary to send a confirmation reply.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at the phone number you text to submit change orders.
